# Product and market research: engineering decisions

## Retain sources

Captured text, source identifiers, timestamps and hashes remain attached to a research run. A quoted citation must resolve to a retained capture. Quote presence alone does not prove that a claim follows from it.

## Represent uncertainty

Reviews retain supported, conflicted and unknown assessments. Missing or failed searches remain visible, and existing product context supplies hypotheses rather than overruling contradictory sources.

## Bound research

The shared workflow limits queries and rounds, rechecks spending constraints and records a stopping reason. Provider outages are distinct from findings. These implementation rules are not a live research-quality benchmark.
